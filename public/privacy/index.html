Privacy Policy

How your home data stays yours. Lumen is built around local control, clear permission prompts, and no advertising or tracking SDKs.

Last updated October 15, 2026 Lumen for iPhone Version 1.0

Overview

Lumen is a home companion for iPhone that organises rooms, devices, and scenes, and explains its suggestions before anything runs. It is built to keep your home context on your device.

What we collect

We do not collect, sell, or transmit your personal home data to servers we operate. Rooms, devices, scenes, automations, and usage context are handled locally on your device. CloudKit sync is disabled in the beta.

If you join the beta waitlist on this site, we store the email address you submit so we can send you a TestFlight invite. We use it only for your beta request and related updates, with delivery providers as described below.

Permissions

Lumen may ask for access to your smart-home configuration, your location for presence features, and notifications for automation updates. Each permission is used only for the feature you enabled it for, and you can withdraw any of them in iOS Settings.

Smart home data

Device and scene information is read so Lumen can render controls, explain a suggestion, and preview an action before it changes anything. That data is not sent to advertising networks, analytics brokers, or profiling services.

Sync

CloudKit sync is currently disabled in the beta. Home context is stored locally on your device. We will update this policy before enabling a new sync service.

Third-party services

The native app contains no third-party advertising or behavioural analytics SDKs. This website keeps anonymous signup outcome events in browser memory; these events do not contain your email address.

Website waitlist

When you join the waitlist, we use your email address and the request source to manage beta invitations and related updates. This is separate from your home data. The website sends your request to our signup endpoint. Depending on its configuration, that endpoint may deliver your request through a private webhook, Supabase, Resend, Web3Forms, or FormSubmit. These services process the request for storage or email delivery. No home data is included. Contact us to request removal from the waitlist.

A successful signup message means the configured delivery service accepted the request. It does not guarantee immediate beta access or that a later invitation will reach your inbox. If delivery cannot be confirmed, the website shows an error and keeps your email available for retry.

Children

Lumen does not knowingly collect personal data from anyone, children under 13 included.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes we will update the date at the top of this page. Material changes will also be mentioned in the release notes for the build they ship with.
